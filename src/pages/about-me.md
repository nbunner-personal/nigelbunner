Hello, I'm a frontend engineer, musician and author.

I've been building and designing websites for over 20 years, the last 11 of them at Tripadvisor. Most recently I was a core engineer on [Cruise Critic](https://www.cruisecritic.com/), one of the world's largest cruise review and planning sites, where I worked on almost every part of the frontend. Before that I was the sole designer and frontend developer on Holiday Watchdog, a UK travel site. You can see some of the sites I've built on my [web portfolio](/web-portfolio.html) page.

I specialise in React, Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind, with a designer's eye for detail. I'm comfortable taking a feature from a Figma design to production, running it as an A/B test and rolling it out.

My skills include:

- **Languages:** TypeScript, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, SASS, PHP and GraphQL
- **Frameworks:** React, Next.js (App Router and Server Components), VueJS, Laravel and Eleventy
- **Styling and UI:** Tailwind CSS, Radix UI, CSS Modules, Styled Components and Storybook, including responsive and print layouts and dark mode
- **Data and state:** Apollo Client, gql.tada, TanStack Query, Zustand and Convex
- **Content and growth:** Storyblok, DatoCMS, feature flags and A/B testing, Adobe Analytics, technical SEO and structured data
- **Quality:** Playwright, Checkly, unit testing, accessibility and Core Web Vitals
- **Tooling:** Git and GitHub, Turborepo, pnpm, Docker, Netlify, and AI coding tools such as Claude Code and Cursor
- **Design:** Figma, Sketch, Photoshop and Illustrator. I can take a design from wireframe to finished product.

I currently have 2 [music](/music.html) projects on the go:

**Sugardrum** is an acoustic setup that I perform live on vocals and acoustic guitar. More info at the [Sugardrum website](https://sugardrum.com).

**Jigsaw Goldrush** is for my more electronic and keyboard-based music. I currently have one album of piano music that has been used for meditation and hypno-birthing. More info at the [Jigsaw Goldrush website](https://www.jigsawgoldrush.com).

More details about my debut book of short stories can be found on the [stories](/stories.html) page.

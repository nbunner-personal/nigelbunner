--- layout: glossary title: What is a domain name? meta-title: What is a domain name? permalink: /glossary/domain-name.html ---

A domain name is the main part of a website's address, or URL, shown in the address bar at the top of your browser window.

The domain name for this site is nigelbunner.co.uk. Think of it like a postal address — you type the domain name into your browser and it takes you to that particular website.

You need to register a domain name for your website so that people know where to find it. You rent it by the year from a domain registrar, and prices vary depending on the ending, for example .co.uk or .com. Some hosting companies include a domain name when you buy hosting from them.
